# MODUL SESI 2: Bagian A Lanjutan + Pengenalan Bagian B (Reading)
**English Olympiad — EAC 2026 | Sesi 2 dari 7 | Durasi: ~105–120 menit**

*Catatan: modul ini disusun dengan asumsi progres standar dari Sesi 1. Kalau pas Sesi 1 kemarin adik masih kesulitan di tenses/nouns dasar, luangkan 10-15 menit ekstra di awal untuk mengulang itu dulu sebelum masuk materi baru — jangan dipaksakan lanjut kalau fondasinya belum kokoh.*

## 1. Review Quiz Sesi 1 (15 menit)

Kerjakan dulu tanpa lihat catatan, baru dibahas.

1. Plural of "man": (mans / men / mans's / manes)
2. My mother ___ dinner every evening. (cook / cooks / cooking / cooked)
3. Yesterday, we ___ a movie together. (watch / watches / watched / watching)
4. Listen! The baby ___. (cry / cries / is crying / cried)
5. We need ___ furniture for the new house. (many / much / few / a few)
6. I like tea, ___ my brother likes coffee. (and / but / so / because)
7. The word "quickly" is a/an: (noun / verb / adjective / adverb)
8. Choose the correct plural of "foot": (foots / feet / footes / feets)
9. She stayed up late ___ she had an exam the next day. (and / but / because / or)
10. He is ___ (careful) when he crosses the street. (care / careful / carefully / carelessness)

**Kunci:** 1-men, 2-cooks, 3-watched, 4-is crying, 5-much, 6-but, 7-adverb, 8-feet, 9-because, 10-careful

*Kalau masih banyak salah (di bawah 6 benar), tandai topik yang lemah dan selipkan 5-10 menit review singkat sebelum lanjut — jangan buru-buru ke materi baru.*

## 2. Materi: Comparative & Superlative (20 menit)

**Adjective/adverb pendek (1 suku kata):** tambah **-er** (comparative) / **-est** (superlative)
- tall → taller → tallest
- big → bigger → biggest *(konsonan dobel karena vokal pendek + 1 konsonan)*
- nice → nicer → nicest *(hilangkan -e sebelum tambah akhiran)*

**Berakhiran -y (2 suku kata):** ganti y → i, tambah -er/-est
- happy → happier → happiest
- easy → easier → easiest

**Kata panjang (2+ suku kata):** pakai **more/most**
- beautiful → more beautiful → most beautiful
- difficult → more difficult → most difficult

**Bentuk tidak beraturan (wajib hafal):**
| Dasar | Comparative | Superlative |
|---|---|---|
| good | better | best |
| bad | worse | worst |
| far | farther/further | farthest/furthest |
| little | less | least |
| many/much | more | most |

**Struktur:**
- Comparative + **than** → *She is taller than her brother.*
- **The** + superlative → *She is the tallest in her class.*
- Kesetaraan: **as...as** → *This bag is as heavy as that one.*

## 3. Materi: Prepositions Dasar (20 menit)

**Waktu:**
- **in** → bulan, tahun, musim → in July, in 2026, in summer
- **on** → hari, tanggal → on Monday, on July 5th
- **at** → jam spesifik → at 7 o'clock, at noon
- ⚠️ Pengecualian: **at night**

**Tempat:**
- **in** → ruang tertutup/area besar → in the room, in Jakarta
- **on** → permukaan → on the table, on the wall
- **at** → titik spesifik → at the door, at the bus stop

**Arah:**
- **to** (tujuan), **into** (masuk ke dalam), **from** (asal), **out of** (keluar dari)
- *He walked into the classroom and sat down.*

---

## 4. Materi: Intro Relative Clause — who & which (10 menit)

Relative clause menggabungkan 2 kalimat jadi 1, memberi info tambahan tentang noun sebelumnya.

- **who** → untuk orang: *The student **who** studies hard will succeed.*
- **which** → untuk benda/hewan: *The book **which** is on the table is mine.*

Latihan gabung kalimat bareng adik:
- *I know a girl. She won the competition.* → *I know a girl who won the competition.*
- *This is the pen. I bought it yesterday.* → *This is the pen which I bought yesterday.*

*(that akan dibahas lebih dalam di sesi mendatang — untuk sekarang cukup kenal pola who/which)*

## 5. Latihan Soal Bagian A Gabungan (25 menit)

1. This bag is ___ than that one. (heavy / heavier / heaviest / more heavy)
2. She is the ___ student in our class. (smart / smarter / smartest / more smart)
3. Jakarta is ___ than Bandung in terms of population. (big / bigger / biggest / more big)
4. This is the ___ movie I have ever watched. (good / better / best / goodest)
5. My score this month is ___ than last month. (bad / worse / worst / more bad)
6. The meeting will be held ___ Monday morning. (in / on / at / to)
7. We usually have breakfast ___ 6 o'clock. (in / on / at / to)
8. She was born ___ 2010. (in / on / at / to)
9. The keys are ___ the table. (in / on / at / into)
10. He walked ___ the classroom and sat down. (in / on / at / into)
11. The student ___ won first place studies every day. (which / who / whose / whom)
12. This is the book ___ I borrowed from the library. (who / whom / which / whose)
13. Choose the synonym of "difficult": (easy / simple / challenging / boring)
14. Choose the antonym of "increase": (rise / grow / decrease / expand)
15. Every Sunday, my family ___ to the market. (go / goes / going / went)

**Kunci Jawaban:**
1. heavier — comparative, diikuti "than"
2. smartest — superlative, pakai "the"
3. bigger — comparative pendek + -er
4. best — superlative tidak beraturan dari "good"
5. worse — comparative tidak beraturan dari "bad"
6. on — hari pakai "on"
7. at — jam spesifik pakai "at"
8. in — tahun pakai "in"
9. on — permukaan meja
10. into — gerakan masuk ke dalam ruangan
11. who — untuk orang (the student)
12. which — untuk benda (the book)
13. challenging — makna serupa dengan "difficult"
14. decrease — lawan kata dari "increase"
15. goes — simple present, subjek tunggal (my family) +s

## 6. Materi & Latihan Bagian B: Strategi Reading (25 menit)

**4 strategi dasar:**
1. **Skimming** — baca cepat untuk tangkap gambaran umum/main idea, jangan baca kata per kata
2. **Scanning** — cari info spesifik (nama, angka, tanggal) tanpa baca semua paragraf
3. **Main idea** — biasanya ada di kalimat pertama atau terakhir paragraf
4. **Context clues** — tebak makna kata asing dari kalimat sebelum/sesudahnya, bukan dari kamus dulu

**Latihan — baca passage berikut (5 menit membaca), lalu jawab tanpa membaca ulang seluruh teks:**

> Budi used to think that joining an English competition was only for students who were already fluent. However, his English teacher told him that consistent practice matters more than natural talent. Motivated by this advice, Budi started studying vocabulary flashcards every morning before school. After several weeks, he noticed that reading English articles became less exhausting than before. His confidence grew, and he decided to register for the upcoming English Olympiad, believing that his hard work would eventually pay off.

1. What is the main idea of the passage?
 A. Budi is naturally talented at English
 B. Budi's teacher discouraged him from competing
 C. Consistent practice helped Budi improve and gain confidence
 D. Budi decided not to join the competition

2. Why did Budi start studying vocabulary flashcards?
 A. He was motivated by his teacher's advice
 B. His friend asked him to
 C. He was forced by his parents
 D. He wanted to skip class

3. The word "exhausting" in the passage is closest in meaning to:
 A. exciting
 B. interesting
 C. confusing
 D. tiring

4. What happened after Budi practiced for several weeks?
 A. He gave up
 B. He stopped studying
 C. Reading became easier for him
 D. He failed a test

5. Based on the passage, we can conclude that:
 A. Talent matters more than practice
 B. Budi never intended to compete
 C. Budi's teacher was unhelpful
 D. Consistent effort can lead to improvement

**Kunci:** 1-C, 2-A, 3-D, 4-C, 5-D

*Tips ngajarin: minta adik tunjuk kalimat persisnya yang jadi bukti jawaban — ini melatih dia untuk selalu kembali ke teks, bukan asal tebak.*
