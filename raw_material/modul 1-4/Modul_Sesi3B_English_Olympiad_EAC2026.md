# MODUL SESI 3B: Menutup Gap Kisi-Kisi Preliminary
**English Olympiad — EAC 2026 | Sesi Tambahan (antara Sesi 3 dan Sesi 4) | Durasi: ~70–80 menit**

> Modul ini isinya 4 topik yang resmi masuk kisi-kisi Preliminary tapi belum kesentuh khusus di Sesi 1-3: idiom & figurative language (Bagian B), cause-effect (Bagian B), relative clause "that" (Bagian A), dan multi-error analysis level dasar (Bagian D). ## 1. Materi: Cause-Effect (15 menit)

Di Bagian B, sering ada soal yang nanya "apa penyebabnya?" atau "apa akibatnya?" dari suatu kejadian di bacaan.

**Kata kunci penanda cause (sebab):** because, since, as, due to
**Kata kunci penanda effect (akibat):** so, therefore, as a result, consequently, thus

**Contoh:**
- *Because the road was flooded, the bus arrived late.* → Sebab: jalan banjir. Akibat: bus terlambat.
- *She studied hard; as a result, she became fluent in English.* → Sebab: rajin belajar. Akibat: jadi fasih.

**Strategi:** begitu ketemu kata kunci ini di bacaan, garis bawahi — itu biasanya kunci jawaban soal cause-effect.

## 2. Materi: Idiom & Figurative Language Dasar (15 menit)

**Idiom umum yang sering muncul di reading:**
| Idiom | Arti |
|---|---|
| cost an arm and a leg | sangat mahal |
| let the cat out of the bag | membocorkan rahasia |
| call it a day | mengakhiri pekerjaan/kegiatan |
| hit the sack | pergi tidur |
| under the weather | merasa kurang sehat |
| keep an eye on | mengawasi |

**Figurative language (review + contoh baru):**
- **Simile** (pakai like/as): *His voice was as smooth as silk.*
- **Metaphor** (tanpa like/as): *The classroom was a zoo.*
- **Personification** (benda mati diberi sifat manusia): *The stars winked at us from the sky.*

**Strategi:** jangan diterjemahkan kata per kata — lihat konteks kalimat sekitar untuk menebak makna keseluruhan.

## 3. Materi: Relative Clause "that" (10 menit)

"**That**" bisa menggantikan "who" (untuk orang) atau "which" (untuk benda) dalam klausa yang menerangkan/membatasi (defining clause) — dan sangat umum dipakai, termasuk di soal ujian.

**Contoh:**
- *This is the restaurant that serves the best noodles in town.* (that = which)
- *The teacher that taught us last year has moved.* (that = who)
- *I lost the pen that my father gave me.* (that = which)

**Catatan:** "that" tidak dipakai setelah tanda koma (untuk klausa non-membatasi/tambahan info) — tapi untuk level Preliminary, cukup fokus dulu ke penggunaan dasar di atas.

## 4. Materi: Multi-Error Analysis Level Preliminary (10 menit)

Sama seperti yang dibahas di Sesi 4, tapi di level yang lebih dasar — fokus ke kesalahan tense dan subject-verb agreement yang paling sering muncul.

**Contoh:**
Which sentence is grammatically correct?
A) Yesterday, I go to the market and buy some vegetables.
B) Yesterday, I went to the market and buy some vegetables.
C) Yesterday, I went to the market and bought some vegetables.
D) Yesterday, I going to the market and bought some vegetables.

Analisis: A salah (present tense, harusnya past), B salah (verb kedua belum diubah ke past), D salah (going tanpa auxiliary). **Jawaban: C** — kedua kata kerja harus konsisten past tense.

## 5. Latihan Soal Gabungan (15 menit)

**Cause-Effect:**
1. "Because the road was flooded, the bus arrived late." Apa penyebabnya? (A. Bus terlambat B. Jalan banjir C. Supir lambat D. Penumpang komplain)
2. "She practiced every day; as a result, she became fluent in English." Apa akibatnya? (A. Rajin latihan B. Jadi fasih bahasa Inggris C. Berhenti latihan D. Kesulitan bahasa Inggris)
3. Kata mana yang menandakan hubungan cause-effect? (A. however B. therefore C. meanwhile D. also)

**Idiom & Figurative Language:**
4. "This laptop cost an arm and a leg." Artinya: (A. sangat murah B. sangat mahal C. rusak D. gratis)
5. "Please don't let the cat out of the bag." Artinya: (A. jangan terlambat B. jangan bocorkan rahasia C. jangan bawa hewan D. jangan marah)
6. "The stars winked at us from the sky." Ini contoh: (A. simile B. metaphor C. personification D. idiom)
7. "His voice was as smooth as silk." Ini contoh: (A. simile B. metaphor C. personification D. idiom)

**Relative Clause "that":**
8. This is the restaurant ___ serves the best noodles in town. (who / whom / that / whose)
9. The teacher ___ taught us last year has moved to another school. (which / that / whose / whom)
10. I lost the pen ___ my father gave me. (who / that / whom / whose)

**Multi-Error Analysis:**
11. Which sentence is correct? A) Yesterday, I go to the market and buy some vegetables. B) Yesterday, I went to the market and buy some vegetables. C) Yesterday, I went to the market and bought some vegetables. D) Yesterday, I going to the market and bought some vegetables.
12. Which sentence is correct? A) The students is studying for their exam. B) The students are studying for their exam. C) The students am studying for their exam. D) The student are studying for their exam.
13. Which sentence is correct? A) She always study English on weekend. B) She always studies English on weekends. C) She always studying English on weekends. D) She always studied English on weekends now.

**Kunci Jawaban:**
1. B, 2. B, 3. B, 4. B, 5. B, 6. C, 7. A, 8. that, 9. that, 10. that, 11. C, 12. B, 13. B

## 6. Mini Reading Passage Integratif (10 menit)

*Last week, Dina forgot her umbrella at home. Because it rained heavily on her way back from school, she got completely soaked. When she finally arrived home, her mother said her situation looked like a drowned rat. Although Dina felt annoyed at first, she decided to hit the books that evening instead of complaining, since she had a big test the next day. As a result, she passed the test with a great score.*

1. What caused Dina to get soaked? (A. She jumped into a pool B. It rained heavily and she forgot her umbrella C. She spilled water on herself D. She went swimming)
2. What does "looked like a drowned rat" mean? (A. she looked extremely wet and messy B. she looked happy C. she looked like an animal D. she looked sick)
3. What was the effect of Dina studying that evening? (A. She failed the test B. She passed the test with a great score C. She got sick D. She forgot the test)

**Kunci:** 1-B, 2-A, 3-B
