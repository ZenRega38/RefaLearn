## 2. D-1: Teknik Sentence Unscrambling (25 menit)

### Langkah kerja
1. **Cari subjek** (siapa/apa yang dibicarakan) dan **kata penghubung** (who/which/that/because/although/if)
2. **Cari kata kerja utama** atau auxiliary (is/was/has been/will)
3. **Susun inti S–V–O**, lalu tambahkan keterangan (cara, tempat, waktu) di belakang
4. **Cek posisi kata khusus** (adverb frekuensi, kata negatif, dll.)
5. **Eliminasi lewat opsi:** lihat angka pertama dan terakhir di 4 opsi. Kalau chunk "rarely" tidak mungkin di awal, semua opsi yang diawali angka itu langsung dicoret
6. **Baca ulang hasilnya** dalam hati: masuk akal dan grammar-nya benar?

### Pola yang sering diuji

| Pola | Aturan | Contoh |
|---|---|---|
| Adverb frekuensi (always, never, rarely, often, usually, hardly ever) | **Sebelum** verb utama; **sesudah** be/auxiliary | She *always* drinks tea. / He is *never* late. |
| Keterangan tempat & waktu | Di akhir kalimat (tempat sebelum waktu) | ...at school every day |
| Relative clause | Menempel langsung di belakang noun yang diterangkan | The girl **who won the contest** is my neighbor. |
| Passive | S + be + V3 (+ by pelaku) | The classroom **was cleaned** by the students. |
| Indirect question | Kata tanya + S + V (**tanpa** inversi) | I wonder **where the bank is**. (bukan: where is the bank) |
| so...that | S + be + so + adjective + that + klausa | He was so tired **that** he fell asleep. |
| Frasa preposisi | Jangan memisahkan verb dari objeknya | He finished his homework after dinner. (bukan: finished after dinner his homework) |

### Contoh soal resmi dari kisi-kisi (dibedah dengan eliminasi)
Susun: (1) rarely / (2) the young prodigy / (3) during / (4) makes mistakes / (5) live recitals
Opsi: A) 2-1-4-3-5  B) 1-2-4-3-5  C) 2-4-1-3-5  D) 5-3-2-1-4

- Subjek = "the young prodigy" (2) → yang tidak dimulai angka 2 (B, D) dicoret
- Adverb "rarely" harus **sebelum** verb "makes mistakes" → C salah (verb mendahului adverb)
- Sisa A: *The young prodigy rarely makes mistakes during live recitals.* ✔

## 3. D-2: Idiomatic Preposition (15 menit)

Ini murni hafalan lewat pengulangan. Kelompokkan berdasarkan preposisinya, bukan alfabet:

| Preposisi | Kata yang "menempel" |
|---|---|
| **at** | good at, excellent at, laugh at, smile at, look at |
| **of** | afraid of, proud of, fond of, aware of, capable of, jealous of, tired of, full of, consist of, accuse (someone) of |
| **in** | interested in, succeed in, participate in, believe in, involved in |
| **for** | responsible for, famous for, ready for, wait for, look for, apologize for, thank (someone) for |
| **on** | depend on, rely on, focus on, concentrate on, insist on, congratulate (someone) on |
| **to** | look forward to, similar to, belong to, listen to, kind to, refer to, contribute to, object to |
| **about** | worried about, excited about, sure about, care about, complain about |
| **from** | different from, suffer from, absent from |
| **with** | angry with (orang), satisfied with, pleased with, deal with |

**Jebakan penting:** setelah preposisi, kata kerja berbentuk **V-ing**, bukan to + V.
- ✔ interested in **learning**, good at **solving**, look forward to **seeing**
- ✘ interested in to learn, look forward to see

### Latihan D-2 (8 soal)

3. The success of the project depends ___ teamwork. (of / in / on / at)
4. She apologized ___ her friend for the misunderstanding. (to / from / at / on)
5. The city is famous ___ its historic temples. (for / of / at / to)
6. My teacher is very kind ___ all of us. (with / to / at / for)
7. This medicine consists ___ three natural herbs. (of / from / in / with)
8. She succeeded ___ passing the entrance test. (in / at / on / for)

**Kunci:** 1-of, 2-to, 3-on, 4-to, 5-for, 6-to, 7-of, 8-in

## 4. D-3: Multi-Error Analysis (15 menit)

Soal bentuknya 4 kalimat mirip, hanya 1 yang bersih dari kesalahan. Tiap kalimat salah punya kesalahan **berbeda**. Jangan pilih yang "kedengarannya enak", periksa satu per satu dengan checklist **STAPW**:

| Huruf | Cek apa | Contoh jebakan |
|---|---|---|
| **S** | **S**ubject–verb agreement | *The children **plays*** / *Every student **have*** |
| **T** | **T**ense (lihat penanda waktu) | *She **has visited** her grandmother **last week*** |
| **A** | **A**rticle, plural, uncountable | *An **informations** about...* |
| **P** | **P**reposisi & bentuk setelahnya | *interested **on** learning* / *look forward **to see*** |
| **W** | **W**ord form (noun / adjective / adverb) | *answered **confident*** / *feels **nervously*** |

**Cara kerja:** baca tiap opsi → coret jika ketemu 1 pelanggaran STAPW → kalau tersisa 2 opsi, cek ulang huruf demi huruf.

### Latihan D-3 (6 soal)
Mana kalimat yang benar secara grammar?

1. A) The children plays football in the yard every afternoon. B) The children play footballs in the yard every afternoon. C) The children play football in the yard every afternoon. D) The children is playing football in the yard every afternoon.
2. A) She has visited her grandmother last week. B) She visited her grandmother last week. C) She has visit her grandmother last week. D) She visits her grandmother last week.
3. A) He answered the question confident. B) He answered the question confidently. C) He answered the question confidence. D) He answered the question confidently, but he feels nervously.
4. A) An information about the competition are available online. B) The information about the competition is available online. C) The informations about the competition is available online. D) The information about the competition are available online.
5. A) She is interested in to learn new languages. B) She is interested on learning new languages. C) She is interested in learning new languages. D) She is interesting in learning new languages.
6. A) If it will rain tomorrow, we will cancel the picnic. B) If it rains tomorrow, we will cancel the picnic. C) If it rained tomorrow, we will cancel the picnic. D) If it rains tomorrow, we would cancelled the picnic.

**Kunci & jejak kesalahan:**
1. **C** — A (S), B (A: footballs), D (S)
2. **B** — A & C (T/form), D (T)
3. **B** — A (W), C (W), D (W: feels nervously)
4. **B** — A (A + S), C (A + S), D (S: information tidak jamak)
5. **C** — A & B (P), D (W: interesting → interested)
6. **B** — A (T: will setelah if), C (T), D (T: would cancelled)

| # | Jebakan | Aturan cepat |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Subject–verb agreement** | he/she/it + verb-s. *Each/every/everyone* + singular. *There is/are* mengikuti noun **sesudahnya** |
| 2 | **Penanda waktu tense** | yesterday/last/ago → past. now/Look! → continuous. every/usually → present. since/for (masih berlanjut) → present perfect |
| 3 | **Irregular verbs** | Wajib hafal (tabel di bawah) |

## 6. Bonus Cheat Sheet: 3 Topik A yang Belum Pernah Diajarkan (5 menit)

Ini topik yang wajar muncul di Bagian A tapi belum sempat dibahas di Sesi 1–3. Cukup dikenali polanya:

- **Passive voice:** be + V3 (+ by pelaku) → *The bridge **was built** in 1990. English **is spoken** in many countries.*
- **Future:** will + V1 (prediksi/keputusan spontan); be going to + V1 (rencana) → *We **will take** a trip next year.*
- **Present perfect (pengenalan):** have/has + V3, untuk pengalaman atau yang baru selesai. **Jangan** dipakai dengan yesterday/last week/ago → *She **has seen** this movie three times.*

**Mini soal bonus:**
1. This bridge ___ in 1990. (built / was built / is building / builds)
2. Next year, we ___ a trip to Japan. (will take / took / take / are took)
3. English ___ in many countries around the world. (speaks / is spoken / speaking / is speaking)
4. She ___ this movie three times. (see / saw / has seen / seeing)

**Kunci:** 1-was built, 2-will take, 3-is spoken, 4-has seen
