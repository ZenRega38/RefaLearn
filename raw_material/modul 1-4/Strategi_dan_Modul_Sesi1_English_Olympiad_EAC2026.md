## 1. Diagnostic Quick Check (10 menit)

Suruh adik jawab cepat, **jangan dibahas dulu** — cuma untuk tahu titik lemahnya di mana. Catat berapa yang benar.

1. She ___ to school every day. (go / goes / going / gone)
2. Plural of "child": (childs / children / childes / child's)
3. I ___ my homework yesterday. (do / did / does / doing)
4. Please give me some ___. (advices / advice / advise / advisement)
5. The beautiful garden is behind the house. Which word is the adjective? (beautiful / garden / behind / house)
6. Look! She ___ (write) a letter now. (write / writes / is writing / wrote)
7. I stayed home ___ it was raining. (but / so / because / or)
8. Synonym of "happy": (sad / joyful / angry / tired)
9. There ___ many students in the class. (is / are / was / be)
10. Antonym of "difficult": (simple / hard / complex / tough)

**Kunci jawaban:** 1-goes, 2-children, 3-did, 4-advice, 5-beautiful, 6-is writing, 7-because, 8-joyful, 9-are, 10-simple

> Kalau adik benar 7-10: fondasinya lebih baik dari perkiraan, sesi 2 bisa dipercepat.
> Kalau benar 4-6: sesuai perkiraan level dasar, ikuti modul apa adanya.
> Kalau benar 0-3: perlu tambahan waktu di tenses & nouns sebelum lanjut ke sesi 2.

## 2. Overview Format & Strategi (10 menit)

Jelaskan ke adik secara singkat:
- Ujian Preliminary: 60 menit, 40 soal pilihan ganda, 4 opsi
- **Tidak ada sistem minus** — kosong = 0, salah = 0, jadi **isi semua soal, jangan ada yang kosong**
- Bisa loncat soal dan balik lagi selama waktu masih ada — kerjakan yang gampang dulu
- 4 bagian: A (vocab/grammar), B (reading), C (dialog), D (HOTS/logika)
- Strategi kita: kuasai Bagian A dulu sampai mendekati sempurna, karena itu paling cepat dilatih dan bobotnya paling besar

## 3. Materi: Nouns — Countable & Uncountable (20 menit)

**Countable nouns** (bisa dihitung, punya bentuk jamak):
- Aturan reguler: tambah **-s** → book → books, cat → cats
- Kata berakhiran -s, -x, -ch, -sh, -o: tambah **-es** → box → boxes, watch → watches, potato → potatoes
- Kata berakhiran konsonan + y: ganti y jadi **-ies** → city → cities, baby → babies
- Bentuk jamak tidak beraturan (wajib dihafal, sering muncul di soal):
  - child → **children**
  - man → **men**, woman → **women**
  - mouse → **mice**, tooth → **teeth**, foot → **feet**
  - person → **people**

**Uncountable nouns** (tidak bisa dihitung, TIDAK punya bentuk jamak — sering jadi jebakan soal):
- information, advice, furniture, homework, news, luggage, money, equipment, bread, water
- Pakai "much" atau "a lot of", BUKAN "many" → *much information*, bukan *informations*

**Quantifier cepat:**
- Countable → many, few, a few
- Uncountable → much, little, a little
- Keduanya → some, any, a lot of

## 4. Materi: Tenses Dasar (20 menit)

**Simple Present** — kebiasaan, fakta umum
- Formula: S + V1 (+s/es untuk he/she/it) → *She reads every day.*
- Signal words: always, usually, every day, often
- ⚠️ Jebakan umum: lupa tambah -s untuk subjek tunggal (he/she/it)

**Simple Past** — kejadian selesai di masa lalu
- Formula: S + V2 (kata kerja beraturan +ed, tidak beraturan hafal) → *He watched TV last night. / She went home.*
- Signal words: yesterday, last week, ago, in 2020

**Present Continuous** — sedang terjadi sekarang / bersifat sementara
- Formula: S + am/is/are + V-ing → *They are studying now.*
- Signal words: now, right now, at the moment, Look!/Listen!

**Tips ngajarin:** buat 3 kolom di kertas (Present / Past / Continuous), minta adik isi kalimat sehari-hari dia sendiri di tiap kolom — lebih nempel daripada cuma hafal rumus.

---

## 5. Materi: Parts of Speech & Conjunctions (10 menit)

**4 kelas kata dasar (penting juga untuk Bagian D nanti — sentence unscrambling):**
- **Noun** (kata benda): book, teacher, happiness
- **Verb** (kata kerja): run, study, become
- **Adjective** (kata sifat, menerangkan noun): beautiful, difficult, happy
- **Adverb** (menerangkan verb/adjective, sering berakhiran -ly): quickly, carefully, very

**Conjunctions dasar:**
- and (menambahkan), but (kontras), or (pilihan), so (akibat)
- because (alasan), although (kontras kuat), when (waktu), if (kondisi)
- Contoh: *I stayed home because it was raining.* vs *I stayed home although it wasn't raining.*

## 6. Latihan Soal Bagian A (15 menit + pembahasan)

1. My sister has three ___. (child / childs / children / childrens)
2. We need some ___ before we start the project. (informations / information / informative / inform)
3. Every morning, my father ___ coffee. (drink / drinks / drinking / drank)
4. Last year, they ___ to Bali for vacation. (go / goes / went / going)
5. Please be quiet! The baby ___ now. (sleep / sleeps / is sleeping / slept)
6. There is not ___ sugar left in the jar. (many / much / few / a few)
7. She is a very ___ student; she always finishes her homework. (diligent / diligence / diligently / diligentness)
8. He speaks English ___. (fluent / fluency / fluently / fluentness)
9. I wanted to go out, ___ it started raining. (and / or / but / because)
10. The mice in the barn ate all the ___. (cheese / cheeses / a cheese / the cheeses)
11. My brother ___ his homework right now. (do / does / is doing / did)
12. We stayed inside ___ the weather was terrible. (although / because / but / or)
13. Choose the correct plural: one tooth, two ___. (tooths / teeth / toothes / teeths)
14. This bag is too heavy; I need ___ help. (much / many / a few / some)
15. The word "happiness" is a ___. (verb / noun / adjective / adverb)

**Kunci Jawaban & Penjelasan Singkat:**
1. **children** — bentuk jamak tidak beraturan
2. **information** — uncountable, tidak ada bentuk jamak
3. **drinks** — simple present, subjek "my father" (he/she/it) +s
4. **went** — simple past dari "go", signal word "last year"
5. **is sleeping** — sedang terjadi sekarang, signal "now"
6. **much** — sugar itu uncountable
7. **diligent** — perlu adjective untuk menerangkan "student"
8. **fluently** — perlu adverb untuk menerangkan verb "speaks"
9. **but** — kontras (ingin keluar, tapi hujan)
10. **cheese** — uncountable, tidak ada bentuk jamak
11. **is doing** — sedang terjadi sekarang ("right now")
12. **because** — memberi alasan
13. **teeth** — bentuk jamak tidak beraturan dari "tooth"
14. **some** — bisa dipakai untuk uncountable dalam kalimat positif umum
15. **noun** — "happiness" adalah kata benda abstrak

*Cara pakai: minta adik kerjakan sendiri dulu tanpa lihat kunci (simulasikan tekanan ujian), baru dibahas satu-satu. Untuk yang salah, minta dia jelaskan ulang alasannya pakai kata-katanya sendiri — itu tanda dia benar-benar paham, bukan sekadar hafal jawaban.*

## 7. Preview Reading — Bagian B (10 menit)

Ini baru perkenalan singkat, pembahasan penuh ada di Sesi 2.

**Passage:**
*Rani has always been shy about speaking English in front of others. When her teacher announced the English Olympiad, she almost didn't sign up. But her older sister encouraged her to try, saying that mistakes are part of learning. Rani finally registered, and now she practices every night after dinner. She still feels nervous, but she is proud that she didn't give up on the chance.*

1. What is the main idea of this passage? (A. Rani is a great speaker B. Rani overcame her fear to join a competition C. Rani's teacher is strict D. Rani doesn't like English)
2. Why did Rani almost not sign up? (A. She was busy B. She was shy C. Her teacher forbade it D. She didn't like the competition)
3. The word "encouraged" in the passage is closest in meaning to: (A. stopped B. motivated C. ignored D. confused)

**Kunci:** 1-B, 2-B, 3-B

## Tips untuk Kakak (Pengajar)

- Level dasar butuh **pengulangan**, bukan materi baru terus — kalau di sesi 2 masih banyak salah di tenses, ulangi dulu sebelum lanjut ke materi baru

- Kalau setelah sesi 2-3 progress masih lambat, pertimbangkan realistis: fokus lolos Preliminary dulu adalah kemenangan besar, bukan cuma juara 1 nasional
