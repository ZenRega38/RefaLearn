## 2. Rapid Review Sheet (15 menit)

Cukup dibaca bareng dan dicoret-coret. Tidak perlu dihafal sekali duduk.

### 2.1 Cause–Effect (Bagian B)
- **Sebab:** because, since, as, due to
- **Akibat:** so, therefore, as a result, consequently, thus
- Strategi: begitu ketemu kata penanda, garis bawahi. Soal "why" mencari sebab, soal "what happened as a result" mencari akibat.

### 2.2 Idiom yang sering muncul
| Idiom | Arti |
|---|---|
| turn over a new leaf | memulai lembaran baru / berubah jadi lebih baik |
| a piece of cake | sangat mudah |
| hit the books | belajar dengan giat |
| under the weather | kurang sehat |
| cost an arm and a leg | sangat mahal |
| let the cat out of the bag | membocorkan rahasia |
| call it a day | menyudahi pekerjaan |
| break the ice | mencairkan suasana / memulai obrolan |

Aturan baku: **jangan menerjemahkan idiom kata per kata**. Lihat kalimat sebelum dan sesudahnya.

### 2.3 Relative clause "that"
- **that** bisa menggantikan **who** (orang) atau **which** (benda) dalam klausa pembatas.
- *The woman **that** lives next door is a doctor.* / *The bag **that** I bought is expensive.*
- Verb setelah relative clause mengikuti **noun yang diterangkan**: *The book **that I borrowed** was interesting.* (bukan were)

### 2.4 Tiga tense/struktur yang wajar muncul di Bagian A
| Struktur | Formula | Penanda |
|---|---|---|
| Passive | be + V3 (+ by pelaku) | *The window was broken by the ball.* |
| Present perfect | have/has + V3 | since (titik waktu), for (durasi), already, just, ever, never |
| Future | will + V1 | tomorrow, next week, if + present |

**Perbedaan since vs for:** *since* + titik waktu (since 2020, since Monday); *for* + durasi (for three years, for an hour).

### 🔒 KUNCI JAWABAN MOCK TEST #2 (jangan dibuka sebelum 60 menit selesai)

**Bagian A:** 1-was built, 2-are cleaned, 3-since, 4-for, 5-will have, 6-confidence, 7-correctly, 8-easier, 9-worst, 10-wrote, 11-that, 12-that, 13-a few, 14-gets, 15-delicate, 16-eager, 17-because, 18-therefore, 19-at, 20-are building

**Bagian B:** 21-C, 22-A, 23-D, 24-C, 25-B, 26-C, 27-D, 28-A, 29-A, 30-C

**Bagian C:** 31-C, 32-B, 33-C, 34-A, 35-D

**Bagian D:** 36-B, 37-C, 38-with, 39-to, 40-D

**Pembahasan singkat soal yang sering jadi jebakan:**
- **1–2:** penanda passive = be + V3. Pelaku dengan "by", objek menjadi subjek.
- **3 vs 4:** *since* + titik waktu/klausa awal (since we were...), *for* + durasi (five years).
- **6–7:** setelah "The" butuh noun (confidence); menerangkan verb "answered" butuh adverb (correctly).
- **11–12:** "that" boleh untuk orang atau benda dalam klausa pembatas. Pada soal 11 "which" salah karena woman = orang.
- **13:** chairs countable, jadi *a few*. Soal 14: *each of* + verb tunggal.
- **26 vs 30:** 26 menanyakan gagasan utama (seluruh teks), 30 menanyakan kesimpulan tersirat.
- **27:** opsi "Lack of flowers only" berbahaya karena ada kata "only". Teks menyebut tiga penyebab.
- **36:** *The students rarely arrive late at school.* Adverb "rarely" sebelum verb.
- **37:** *This device was invented by a young engineer in 2019.* Opsi 3-1-4-2 (in 2019 by...) sebenarnya juga masuk akal, tapi tidak ada di pilihan. Pelaku (by) biasanya langsung setelah verb passive.
- **38–39:** satisfied **with**, belong **to**. Preposisi menempel pada kata sebelumnya, jadi harus dihafal.
- **40:** A salah (likes, eat), B salah (don't), C salah (eating). Cek dengan STAPW dari Sesi 3C.

**Cara hitung skor kasar (skala 100):**
- Bagian A: jumlah benar × 1,75 (maks 35)
- Bagian B: jumlah benar × 2,7 (maks 27)
- Bagian C: jumlah benar × 3,2 (maks 16)
- Bagian D: jumlah benar × 4,4 (maks 22)

## 5. Lab D Tambahan (10 soal, PR bertahap)

Kerjakan dalam 2 hari, pakai timer, lalu bandingkan dengan kunci.

**Unscrambling**
1. Susun: (1) is very patient / (2) the teacher / (3) explained the lesson / (4) who
 A) 2-4-1-3 B) 2-1-4-3 C) 2-4-3-1 D) 4-2-3-1
2. Susun: (1) always / (2) are / (3) the stairs / (4) crowded / (5) in the morning
 A) 3-1-2-4-5 B) 1-3-2-4-5 C) 3-2-4-1-5 D) 3-2-1-4-5
3. Susun: (1) that / (2) he could not speak / (3) the runner / (4) was so exhausted
 A) 3-4-1-2 B) 3-1-4-2 C) 1-3-4-2 D) 3-4-2-1
4. Susun: (1) me / (2) could you tell / (3) the post office / (4) is / (5) where
 A) 2-1-5-4-3 B) 5-2-1-3-4 C) 2-1-5-3-4 D) 2-5-1-3-4

**Idiomatic preposition**
5. She is tired ___ doing the same task every day. (of / at / on / with)
6. He relied ___ his notes during the discussion. (at / on / for / of)
7. The teacher thanked the students ___ their help. (to / of / at / for)

**Multi-error**
8. A) Every morning, my father drink coffee and read the newspaper. B) Every morning, my father drinks coffee and reading the newspaper. C) Every morning, my father drinks coffee and reads the newspaper. D) Every morning, my fathers drinks coffee and reads the newspaper.
9. A) The book that I borrowed from the library was very interesting. B) The book that I borrowed from the library were very interesting. C) The book who I borrowed from the library was very interesting. D) The book that I borrowed from the library was very interestingly.
10. A) Although she was tired, but she finished the report. B) Although she tired, she finished the report. C) Although she was tired, she finish the report. D) Although she was tired, she finished the report.

**Kunci Lab D:** 1-C, 2-D, 3-A, 4-C, 5-of, 6-on, 7-for, 8-C, 9-A, 10-D

**Alasan singkat:**
1. *The teacher who explained the lesson is very patient.* Relative clause menempel di noun.
2. *The stairs are always crowded in the morning.* Adverb frekuensi setelah "are".
3. *The runner was so exhausted that he could not speak.* Pola so...that.
4. *Could you tell me where the post office is?* Indirect question tanpa inversi.
8. A salah (drink, read), B salah (reading harus paralel: reads), D salah (fathers).
9. B salah (subjek "book" singular, jadi was), C salah (who untuk benda), D salah (adverb setelah "was").
10. "Although" dan "but" tidak dipakai bersamaan di satu kalimat.

## 7. Strategi Hari-H (dibaca ulang malam sebelum ujian)

**Urutan pengerjaan yang disarankan** (adik kuat di C dan B, jadi dua bagian itu "menabung" waktu dan poin):

| Bagian | Waktu | Catatan |
|---|---|---|
| C (5 soal) | ±4 menit | Kerjakan dulu, poin aman |
| B (10 soal) | ±14 menit | Skim passage dulu, cari bukti di teks |
| A (20 soal) | ±18 menit | Tandai soal ragu, lanjut dulu |
| D (5 soal) | ±18 menit | Eliminasi opsi, pakai STAPW |
| Cek ulang | ±6 menit | Kembali ke soal bertanda |

**Aturan emas:**
1. **Semua soal harus terisi.** Salah atau kosong sama-sama 0 poin, tidak ada pengurangan nilai.
2. Soal yang bikin macet lebih dari 1,5 menit: tandai, pindah, kembali nanti.
3. Soal unscrambling: eliminasi dulu lewat angka pertama opsi (subjek harus di depan), lalu cek posisi adverb.
4. Soal preposisi: jangan terjemahkan dari bahasa Indonesia, ingat pasangan katanya.
5. Jangan ganti jawaban tanpa alasan grammar yang jelas.
6. Peserta mendapat kertas buram untuk coretan. Gunakan untuk menomori ulang chunk di soal unscrambling.

**Checklist malam sebelumnya:**
- [ ] Perangkat, koneksi, dan alat tulis (pulpen/pensil) siap sesuai ketentuan
- [ ] Cek email atau info panitia untuk akses dan teknis ujian
- [ ] Review ringan saja, tidak belajar materi baru
- [ ] Tidur cukup
