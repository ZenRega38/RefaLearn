## 2. Materi: Present Perfect & Past Perfect (20 menit)

**Present Perfect**
Formula: **S + have/has + V3** (past participle)

Dipakai untuk:
- Pengalaman tanpa waktu spesifik → *I have visited Bali.*
- Aksi yang mulai di masa lalu dan masih berlanjut → *She has lived here for five years.*
- Aksi yang baru saja selesai → *He has just finished his homework.*

Signal words: already, just, yet, ever, never, since, for

**Perbedaan penting dengan Simple Past:**
| Simple Past | Present Perfect |
|---|---|
| Waktu spesifik disebutkan | Waktu tidak spesifik / masih relevan sekarang |
| *I visited Bali last year.* | *I have visited Bali.* |

**Past Perfect** (pengenalan)
Formula: **S + had + V3**
Dipakai untuk aksi yang terjadi **sebelum** aksi lain di masa lalu.
Contoh: *By the time she arrived, the movie had already started.*

## 3. Materi: HOTS Lanjutan — Multi-Error Analysis (15 menit)

Format soal ini muncul di Bagian D: 4 pilihan kalimat, hanya 1 yang benar sepenuhnya secara grammar — sisanya punya kesalahan berbeda-beda (verb form, subject-verb agreement, tense, dll). Tugasnya: temukan kesalahan di TIAP opsi, baru pilih yang benar.

**Contoh:**
Which sentence is grammatically correct?
A) She don't like spicy food.
B) She doesn't likes spicy food.
C) She doesn't like spicy food.
D) She not like spicy food.

Cara analisis: A salah (don't → doesn't untuk subjek "she"), B salah (likes seharusnya like setelah "doesn't"), D salah (butuh auxiliary "doesn't"). **Jawaban: C**

**Strategi:** jangan cuma cari yang "kedengarannya benar" — cek satu-satu: subject-verb agreement, bentuk kata kerja (V1/V2/V3), dan penggunaan auxiliary verb.

## 4. Materi: Idiom & Figurative Language (15 menit)

**Idiom yang sering muncul di reading passage:**
| Idiom | Arti |
|---|---|
| break the ice | memulai percakapan |
| a fish out of water | merasa tidak nyaman di situasi baru |
| piece of cake | sangat mudah |
| once in a blue moon | jarang terjadi |
| hit the books | belajar dengan giat |
| give up | menyerah |

**Figurative language:**
- **Simile** — perbandingan pakai "like/as" → *as brave as a lion*
- **Metaphor** — perbandingan langsung tanpa "like/as" → *time is money*
- **Personification** — benda mati diberi sifat manusia → *the wind whispered*

**Strategi di Bagian B:** kalau ketemu idiom/ungkapan asing, jangan diterjemahkan kata per kata — lihat konteks kalimat sekitarnya untuk menebak maknanya.

## 5. Latihan Soal Gabungan Level Lanjut (25 menit)

1. She ___ her homework already. (finish / finished / has finished / finishing)
2. By the time we arrived, the train ___. (leave / left / has left / had left)
3. I have never ___ such a delicious cake before. (eat / ate / eaten / eating)
4. They ___ in this city since 2015. (live / lived / has lived / have lived)
5. Which sentence is grammatically correct?
 A) He have finished his project already.
 B) He has finished his project already.
 C) He has finish his project already.
 D) He finish his project already.
6. Which sentence is grammatically correct?
 A) She don't like spicy food.
 B) She doesn't likes spicy food.
 C) She doesn't like spicy food.
 D) She not like spicy food.
7. "It's raining cats and dogs" means: (raining lightly / raining very heavily / animals are falling / it's sunny)
8. The phrase "a fish out of water" means: (very happy / feeling out of place / very cold / outside in the rain)
9. "Time is money" is an example of: (simile / metaphor / personification / idiom)
10. He is proud ___ his achievement. (of / at / for / in)
11. She is afraid ___ spiders. (of / at / for / in)
12. My uncle depends ___ his savings for retirement. (on / at / for / in)
13. Choose the correct sentence:
 A) If I will have time, I will call you.
 B) If I have time, I will call you.
 C) If I have time, I call you.
 D) If I had time, I will call you.
14. Choose the synonym of "diligent": (lazy / hardworking / careless / quiet)
15. Choose the antonym of "abundant": (plentiful / scarce / huge / many)

**Kunci Jawaban:**
1. has finished — pengalaman/aksi selesai tanpa waktu spesifik
2. had left — aksi sebelum aksi lain di masa lalu (past perfect)
3. eaten — present perfect, "have never + V3"
4. have lived — aksi mulai masa lalu dan masih berlanjut ("since 2015")
5. B — satu-satunya yang benar secara grammar
6. C — satu-satunya yang benar secara grammar
7. raining very heavily — idiom cuaca
8. feeling out of place — makna idiom
9. metaphor — perbandingan langsung tanpa "like/as"
10. of — proud of
11. of — afraid of
12. on — depend on
13. B — conditional type 1: if + simple present, will + V1
14. hardworking — sinonim diligent
15. scarce — antonim abundant

## 6. Reading Passage Level Lanjut (15 menit)

*When Sarah first joined the debate club, she felt like a fish out of water during public speaking sessions. Her hands would shake every time she stood in front of an audience. Her coach told her that practice makes perfect, and encouraged her to hit the books on argumentation techniques every night. Slowly but surely, Sarah's confidence grew. By the end of the semester, she had already won two regional debate competitions. Her journey proves that even the most nervous beginners can become confident speakers if they are willing to put in consistent effort.*

1. What does "a fish out of water" mean in this context?
 A) sick B) confused C) uncomfortable/out of place D) excited
2. What did Sarah's coach encourage her to do?
 A) Join a swimming club B) Quit the debate club C) Speak less D) Study argumentation techniques regularly
3. What happened by the end of the semester?
 A) Sarah gave up B) Sarah's hands stopped shaking immediately C) Sarah changed clubs D) Sarah won two regional debate competitions
4. The phrase "slowly but surely" suggests that Sarah's improvement was:
 A) instant B) random C) gradual but steady D) unsuccessful
5. What is the main message of the passage?
 A) Public speaking is impossible to learn B) Only naturally confident people can debate C) Coaches are not helpful D) Consistent effort can help overcome nervousness

**Kunci:** 1-C, 2-D, 3-D, 4-C, 5-D
